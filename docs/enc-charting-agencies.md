# National charting agencies and their electronic navigational charts

**22 November 2026.** For a downloadable maritime ENC collection with documented reuse rights, NOAA offers 7,114 active US charts in one 772 MB download. For rivers and canals, the US Army Corps of Engineers and several European waterway authorities publish Inland ENCs. The worldwide maritime directory below identifies the issuing offices behind 84 chart prefixes in the licensed ADMIRALTY service. [NOAA downloads](https://charts.noaa.gov/ENCs/ENCs.shtml) · [ADMIRALTY catalogue](https://www.admiralty.co.uk/charts/digital-charts/admiralty-vector-chart-service/updates-support)

An electronic navigational chart (ENC) is an official vector chart issued by or under the authority of a government. Here **open data** means the chart data may be used, adapted and redistributed, including commercially, under published terms or public-domain status. **Free download** describes the price and can carry restrictions on copying, modification or commercial use. **Licensed service** means access through permits, a subscription or an authorised distributor. These apply to a particular product. One country can offer all three. [IHO guide to electronic charts](https://iho.int/uploads/user/pubs/standards/s-66/S-66%20Ed%202.0.0_Final.pdf)

## Downloadable charts with documented reuse rights

### United States maritime charts

**NOAA Office of Coast Survey.** The charts cover US ocean and coastal waters, territories and the Great Lakes, across about 3.6 million square nautical miles. The catalogue valid **21 November 2026** lists **7,114 active cells** and 152 cancelled entries. The all-US ZIP posted 20 November is **772 MB**. Download individual S-57 cells, regional bundles or the national ZIP. NOAA posts new updates on weekday evenings. The XML catalogue gives each cell’s status, compilation scale, edition, update number and ZIP location. [Coverage and update schedule](https://marinenavigation.noaa.gov/charts.html) · [Download charts](https://charts.noaa.gov/ENCs/ENCs.shtml) · [Machine-readable catalogue](https://charts.noaa.gov/ENCs/ENCProdCat.xml)

NOAA permits use and redistribution without a fee. Its agreement describes how to show the agreement and identify NOAA as the source when mirroring or incorporating the data. Official redistribution for regulated vessel carriage requires NOAA distributor certification. A GeoServer portrayal is a derived map. [NOAA ENC user agreement](https://charts.noaa.gov/ENCs/Agreement.shtml)

**National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency (NGA).** The NOAA download service also carries selected NGA charts where the US has charting responsibility overseas, including Haiti, parts of Antarctica and Pacific islands. The 21 November catalogue contains **27 active cells with Haiti in the title**, totalling **5.13 MB** as individual ZIPs. For example, **US5PK4S4** covers Port-au-Prince and Lafiteau, edition 1, update 0, issued 10 December 2024. These cells are already included in the NOAA service total. This public route applies only to the released cells. [NGA’s American Practical Navigator, §517](https://msi.nga.mil/apology_objects/Bowditch_Vol_1.pdf) · [IHO report on NGA’s Haiti charts](https://iho.int/uploads/user/Capacity%20Building/Reports%20Assessments/2017/2017-A06-TV-Haiti-Report.pdf) · [NOAA cell catalogue](https://charts.noaa.gov/ENCs/ENCProdCat.xml) · [Example cell ZIP](https://charts.noaa.gov/ENCs/US5PK4S4.zip)

### United States inland charts

**US Army Corps of Engineers, Army Geospatial Center.** The **109 Inland ENC cells** cover commercial river reaches including the Mississippi, Ohio, Illinois and Tennessee systems. The full S-57 ZIP was **261.6 MB** on 22 November. Individual downloads list river miles, endpoints, edition, posting date and checksum. USACE maintains Inland ECDIS 2.4 charts on a monthly cycle and identifies its chart server as the official distribution source. US federal government works are generally public domain in the US. The separate federal GIS distribution lists CC0. [Chart downloads](https://ienccloud.us/ienc_s57.html) · [Publication cycle](https://ienccloud.us/) · [GIS product terms](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/inland-electronic-navigational-charts) · [US copyright law §105](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html#105)

### European inland charts

**Netherlands — Rijkswaterstaat.** The Dutch inland ENC dataset carries **CC0 1.0**. Three production packages cover the inland network, Zeeland, and the Wadden Sea. The latter two include depth data. In November the national package excluding Zeeland and the Wadden Sea was **42 MB**, Zeeland **47 MB**, and the Wadden Sea **25 MB**. The national file was dated 13 November and the two depth packages were marked week 47. The portal also hosts separate port-authority products with their own publishers. [Dataset and license](https://data.overheid.nl/dataset/3482b76e-d53f-42c4-98a2-7500f708b190) · [Download catalogue](https://www.vaarweginformatie.nl/frp/page/infra_enc) · [Package sizes and dates](https://www.vaarweginformatie.nl/frp/api/webcontent/downloads?pageId=infra/enc)

**Germany — Federal Waterways and Shipping Administration (WSV).** The Rhine, Danube, Elbe, Main and federal canals are available as waterway-specific ZIPs. A 19 November inventory contained **42 packages and 479 base cells**. ELWIS lists river kilometres, issue dates and changes for each reach and offers email notices. WSV releases the data under **GeoNutzV**, which permits commercial and noncommercial reuse. WSV expressly waives the source notice for these Inland ENCs. [WSV catalogue and terms](https://www.elwis.de/DE/Service/Inland-ENC-der-WSV/Inland-ENC-der-WSV-node) · [Permitted uses](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/geonutzv/__2.html) · [Dated inventory](https://github.com/AShapira/geoserver-enc-plugin/blob/9aae85ef75206d6e675dcac47162516009890db7/docs/enc-validation.json)

**Belgium — Service public de Wallonie (SPW).** The Walloon geoportal lists **31 individually named chart ZIPs** for the regional waterway network, including Meuse, Sambre and canal cells. It also offers a GeoPackage for GIS users. The IENC dataset carries **CC BY 4.0**, allowing adaptation and publication with attribution. The record gives **15 March 2018** as its data revision and no scheduled refresh. The newer Belgian EuRIS export below includes other producers as well. [Walloon IENC files, dates and license](https://geoportail.wallonie.be/catalogue/3d57a714-7786-4da0-a144-2813fcc8a0b1.html)

**Czechia — State Navigation Administration (Státní plavební správa).** The Elbe and Vltava system is available in S-57 for Inland ECDIS. EuRIS lists **61 maps, 12.3 MB**. The Czech national catalogue designates the charts as an **open high-value dataset**, lists an annual update frequency, and records no protected original database or database-maker right for the distribution. Individual sheets have identifiers and reach names. [National open-data record](https://data.gov.cz/datov%C3%A1-sada?iri=https%3A%2F%2Fdata.gov.cz%2Fzdroj%2Fdatov%C3%A9-sady%2F00003352%2F1467540066) · [Navigation authority’s geoportal guide](https://geoportal.plavebniurad.cz/web/content/doc/uzivatelska_dokumentace.pdf?v=5)

**Bulgaria — Executive Agency for Exploration and Maintenance of the Danube River (EAEMDR).** The agency’s chart download covers **Danube km 375–610** and was dated **20 November 2026**. The Bulgarian EuRIS export contains **48 maps, 3.3 MB**. EAEMDR permits reuse with visible source credit, requires changes to be identified, and specifies how to describe the period and scope when republishing original data. Its name and logo have separate promotional-use restrictions. [Agency download](https://www.appd-bg.org/mapris-en) · [Reuse terms](https://www.appd-bg.org/pages-en?id=opendata) · [EuRIS country ZIP](https://www.eurisportal.eu/AWFIENC/api/IENC/DownloadIENCExportFile?country=BG)

**Austria — viadonau.** The Austrian Danube and Vienna’s Donaukanal are available from DoRIS. The **31 MB** Inland ECDIS 2.5 package dated **5 November 2026** includes edition 23 topographic cells and a separate bathymetric layer. The national open-data catalogue labels its S-57 ZIP **CC BY 4.0**, while the edition 23 download page prohibits commercial use because it includes third-party survey data. The catalogue license alone is insufficient to establish commercial reuse of that edition. [Open-data distribution](https://www.data.gv.at/datasets/ee58c573-b520-4813-b85a-00a0951df0dd?locale=de) · [Edition 23 files and terms](https://www.doris.bmimi.gv.at/en/inland-encs/downloads/inland-encs-inland-ecdis-standard-25)

## Other public downloads and their limits

**New Zealand — Toitū Te Whenua Land Information New Zealand (LINZ).** The free NZ ENC Service offers **eight regional packs** for New Zealand, parts of the South Pacific and the Ross Sea. Register a compatible navigation system, obtain its cell permits, and apply the published updates. The official ENCs use **S-63 encryption**. For GIS work, LINZ separately publishes chart-derived vector layers through the LINZ Data Service. Those layers do not preserve the official ENC exchange set. [NZ ENC Service](https://www.linz.govt.nz/products-services/charts/nz-enc-service) · [Chart packages](https://encservice.linz.govt.nz/download) · [GIS dataset guidance](https://www.linz.govt.nz/guidance/data-service/linz-data-service-guide/questions-and-answers)

**Poland — Office of Inland Navigation in Szczecin.** Three Inland ECDIS 2.4 exchange sets cover the Oder, Western Oder and Warta. The 9 November listings give roughly **3.8 MB**, **2.45 MB** and **70 kB**, respectively. Charts are free to view or use for navigation. Copying, editing or distributing chart content requires the office’s written consent under its posted terms. [Exchange sets by river](https://www.ris-odra.pl/?lang=pl&page_id=148) · [Conditions of use](https://www.szczecin.uzs.gov.pl/ris-odra/elektroniczne-mapy-nawigacyjne/pobierz-mapy/)

**Switzerland — Port of Switzerland (Schweizerische Rheinhäfen).** One downloadable Upper Rhine cell, **4C7RH149**, covers **20.9 km**, from Rheinfelden at km 149.10 to the Swiss–German border at km 170.00. The listed release is edition 8, update 0, dated 9 December 2025. Significant changes trigger a new release and a notice to inland mariners. The download page provides no express open redistribution license. [Upper Rhine chart and notices](https://port-of-switzerland.ch/hafenservice/schifffahrtsservice/inland-enc-hochrhein/)

**Brazil — Navy Hydrographic Centre (CHM), Directorate of Hydrography and Navigation.** The public inland programme lists **nine prototype cells** for testing on the Tietê, Madeira and Paraguai rivers. Individual ZIPs are **about 103–793 kB** and carry file dates and space for notices. CHM requires prior authorisation for commercial use. Its operational maritime portfolio is a separate licensed product. [Inland prototype catalogue and terms](https://www.marinha.mil.br/chm/dados-do-segnav/cartas-ienc) · [Navy chart products](https://www.marinha.mil.br/chm/dados-do-segnav-cartas-nauticas)

## Worldwide maritime charting agencies

The table includes **every S-57 producer prefix with at least one navigational cell in the ADMIRALTY Vector Chart Service (AVCS) week 47 file for 2026**. There are **27,291 distinct cells under 84 prefixes**. The count uses eight-character cell names in navigation bands 1–6 and excludes the GB800001 information overlay. Each row gives a real cell identifier for catalogue lookup. A prefix can represent a territory, a joint product or one of several offices in the same country. The counts measure this distributor’s holdings. National catalogues can have a different number of active cells. [Week 47 source CSV](https://assets.admiralty.co.uk/public/documents/2026-11/ENC-Update-List-WK47_26.csv?VersionId=.N8GuEtrw9cEtOv7fiGmoxLs9kV57b.s) · [ADMIRALTY support and current files](https://www.admiralty.co.uk/charts/digital-charts/admiralty-vector-chart-service/updates-support) · [IHO producer register](https://registry.iho.int/producercode/list2.do) · [IHO standards index, S-62](https://iho.int/standards-and-specifications)

**Access for every row below is available through the licensed AVCS route.** AVCS customers license the cells they need through an authorised distributor. Some offices also sell directly, and the US and New Zealand offer the direct services described above. A distributor’s navigation permit grants access on its terms. Public WMS display, GIS adaptation and raw-cell redistribution can require a separate license. For an area without a domestic prefix, use the IHO coverage catalogue to identify the issuing office. [AVCS service](https://www.admiralty.co.uk/charts/digital-charts/admiralty-vector-chart-service) · [PRIMAR licensing channels](https://www.primar.org/distributors) · [IHO coverage catalogues](https://iho.int/iho-online-catalogues)

## Using these sources in GeoServer

This project reads **unencrypted S-57 exchange sets**. A base cell ends in `.000`. Keep its numbered updates (`.001`, `.002`, …) beside the base so that the reader can apply them in sequence. The project has named importers for NOAA, USACE, German WSV, Dutch Rijkswaterstaat, and the Belgian and Romanian EuRIS exports. Its WMS and WMTS portrayals are for GIS use. S-63 permit packages and S-101 products need different readers. [Project publishing guide](publishing-guide.md)
